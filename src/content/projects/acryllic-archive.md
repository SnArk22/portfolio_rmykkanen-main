description: A collection of acrylic paintings I've completed over time, exploring a variety of subjects, styles, and artistic techniques. These paintings range from more detailed, representational work to abstract and experimental compositions, providing an opportunity to develop my understanding of color, texture, and composition. Working with acrylic paint has been particularly interesting due to its versatility and relatively fast drying time, allowing for experimentation with layering, blending, and different approaches to applying paint.

Key skills developed through these paintings:

- Acrylic painting techniques
- Color theory and mixing
- Composition and visual balance
- Layering and blending techniques
- Brush control and paint application
- Experimentation with texture and different painting styles

This archive serves as both a collection of finished paintings and a record of my development as an artist, particularly in how my approach to acrylic painting and understanding of the medium have evolved over time.
