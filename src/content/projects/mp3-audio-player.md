---
title: MP3 Audio-Video Player
description: An ongoing project focused on designing and building a portable device capable of playing both audio and video files. The goal of this project is to combine the functionality of a traditional MP3 player with video playback capabilities, while maintaining a relatively simple and intuitive user interface. The project involves experimenting with both hardware and software, particularly in how the two interact to create a functional, self-contained media player.
creator: Ruby Mykkanen
completed: WIP
tags: [Electronics, Programming, Hardware, Design]
---

Key skills being developed during this project:

- Embedded systems development
- Hardware and software integration
- User interface design
- Audio and video playback
- Circuit design and electronic prototyping
- Troubleshooting and iterative design

This project is currently a work in progress, with an emphasis on developing a functional prototype and refining the overall design as different technical challenges arise.
