Key skills explored during this project:

- Basic circuit design and assembly
- Working with electronic components
- Introduction to Arduino programming
- Understanding the relationship between hardware and software
- Reading and following electronic schematics and tutorials
- Troubleshooting basic circuits

While I didn't end up completing everything I had originally planned, this project served as an introduction to practical electronics and helped establish a foundation for exploring more complex hardware and programming projects in the future.
