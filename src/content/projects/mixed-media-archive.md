---
title: Mixed-Media Art Archive
description: A collection of mixed-media artwork I've completed over time, combining different materials, techniques, and artistic approaches within individual pieces. These projects provide an opportunity to experiment with the relationships between different mediums, particularly how variations in texture, color, and composition can influence the appearance and meaning of a piece. Much of this work is relatively experimental, involving a combination of deliberate planning and more spontaneous creative decisions.
creator: Ruby Mykkanen
completed: Ongoing
tags: [Art, Mixed Media, Experimental Art]
---

Key skills developed through these projects:

- Combining different artistic mediums and materials
- Composition and visual balance
- Color theory and experimentation
- Exploring texture and dimensionality
- Creative problem-solving
- Developing cohesive compositions from different materials
- Experimentation with unconventional artistic techniques

This archive serves as both a collection of finished work and a record of my experimentation with different artistic materials and techniques, particularly in how combining mediums can create visual effects that might not be possible when working with a single material.
