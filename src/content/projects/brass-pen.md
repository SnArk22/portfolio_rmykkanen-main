---
title: Brass Pen Machining
description: A machining project in which I fabricated a pen from a solid brass round-stock rod using a metal lathe. Throughout the process, I practiced several fundamental machining operations, including facing, turning, tapping, and threading, to shape the brass stock and produce the components necessary for the pen's assembly. This project provided an opportunity to develop my understanding of precision machining, particularly how different cutting operations can be used to transform raw material into a functional, finished object.
creator: Ruby Mykkanen
completed: n/a
tags: [Machining, Metalworking, Lathe, Fabrication]
---

Key skills learned during this project:

- Operating a metal lathe
- Facing and turning operations
- Internal tapping and threading
- Working with brass round stock
- Precision machining and dimensional accuracy
- Tool selection and cutting techniques
- Planning and executing sequential machining operations

This project provided hands-on experience with several common lathe operations, while reinforcing the importance of precision and careful planning when machining individual components. I found it particularly interesting to work through the process of turning a relatively simple piece of raw material into a functional object.
