title: Bicycle Repair and Restoration
description: A personal project I completed during the summer of 2026, focused on repairing an older bicycle and restoring it to working condition. The project involved replacing a rusted chain, lubricating the new chain, adjusting the brakes and replacing worn brake pads, correcting a misalignment in one of the wheels, and replacing the inner tubes in both tires. Throughout the process, I developed a better understanding of bicycle mechanics, particularly how the different components of a bicycle interact and how to identify and resolve common mechanical problems.
creator: Ruby Mykkanen
completed: Summer 2026
tags: [Mechanics, Repair, Restoration, Bicycle Maintenance]
---

Key skills learned during this project:

- Bicycle maintenance and mechanical troubleshooting
- Chain replacement and lubrication
- Brake adjustment and brake pad replacement
- Diagnosing and correcting wheel misalignment
- Tire removal and inner tube replacement
- Working with hand tools and mechanical components
- Identifying worn or damaged parts and determining appropriate repairs

This project provided an opportunity to gain practical experience with mechanical repair and maintenance, while learning more about the individual systems that allow a bicycle to function properly. I found it particularly interesting to work through the process of identifying mechanical issues and repairing them independently, rather than simply replacing the bicycle.
