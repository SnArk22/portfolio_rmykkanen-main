title: Watercolor Painting Archive
description: A collection of watercolor paintings I've completed over time, exploring a variety of subjects, styles, and techniques. These paintings range from more detailed, representational pieces to looser and more experimental compositions, providing an opportunity to develop my understanding of color, light, and composition. Working with watercolor has been particularly interesting due to the medium's transparency and relative unpredictability, requiring a balance between deliberate planning and allowing the paint to behave naturally.
creator: Ruby Mykkanen
completed: Ongoing
tags: [Art, Watercolor, Painting]
---

Key skills developed through these paintings:

- Watercolor painting techniques
- Color theory and mixing
- Composition and visual balance
- Layering and transparency
- Brush control
- Experimentation with different artistic styles

This archive serves as both a collection of finished work and a record of my development as an artist, particularly in how my approach to watercolor and understanding of the medium have evolved over time.
