---
title: The Incuscope
description: The Incuscope is a low-cost microscopy system designed to perform live-cell imaging inside a standard laboratory incubator. Developed as an accessible alternative to commercial live-cell imaging systems, the project focused on producing a reliable, high-quality instrument while working within the mechanical, electrical, and environmental constraints of an incubator.
creator: Ruby Mykkanen

completed: 8/20/2026

---

During this project, I...

- Designed and modeled mechanical components using Autodesk Inventor and Fusion 360
- Performed mechanical calculations, including torque requirements and component selection
- Worked with actuators, lead screws, support structures, gearboxes, and other mechanical systems
- Learned to design electronic circuits and PCBs using KiCad, as well as fabricate boards using a PCB mill
- Designed components with specific environmental constraints in mind, particularly the temperature and humidity conditions inside a cell incubator
- Collaborated with other researchers throughout the design and development process
- Presented technical findings, design decisions, and project progress during lab meetings
- Machined and fabricated components using a lathe, bandsaw, and milling machine

This project was completed during my time as a researcher in the Varanasi Group at MIT. It provided an opportunity to work through the process of developing a scientific instrument, from initial design and calculations to fabrication and integration, while gaining experience with the practical challenges of research-oriented engineering.
